Description

The Brunswick Smash 7.0 opens for doubles and folds for solo practice. When you need the space back, fold the table and roll it into storage.

Features

The black playing surface has a roller-coated finish over a 4 mm aluminum-clad ABS deck. A silver undercarriage supports the table for indoor or outdoor play.

Dimensions

Care & Maintenance

Cover the table or store it indoors between games. Wipe the playing surface with a soft, damp cloth and mild soap. Avoid abrasive cleaners and scouring pads.
